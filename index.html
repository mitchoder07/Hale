01 / The practice

Time is the most underrated medicine there is.

Most primary care is scheduled in seven minute slices. Nobody gets heard, problems get a prescription instead of a plan, and patients learn to stop mentioning the thing that's actually bothering them.

We built Hale around the opposite bet. Fewer patients per doctor, visits long enough to think in, and a chart that's actually read before you walk in. It turns out that when you give a good doctor an hour, they need fewer tests, fewer referrals, and a lot fewer follow ups.

07 / Fair questions

Asked often.

We're a pay per visit practice with flat published prices, which is usually cheaper than a capped network plan's copays for the same things. Pay with card, FSA, or HSA. Every visit comes with a superbill you can submit to your insurer for out of network reimbursement, and we'll help you fill it out.

Routine visits usually within the week. Sick visits are held back daily, so if you message before 10 am there's a very good chance we see you the same day. Urgent matters get a callback from a doctor, not a triage script, and our average is nine minutes.

Labs are drawn on site and billed at cost, which we show you before the needle comes out. Prescriptions are sent to your pharmacy the same day, and routine refills can be requested with one message. No "call back in three business days."

From age two up. Checkups, growth and development, school and camp forms, and vaccines on schedule. Dr. Raman keeps a sticker drawer of considerable reputation.

We refer to people we'd send our own families to, inside your network whenever possible. Your records go the same day, and we stay in the loop afterwards instead of waving goodbye. A referral from Hale comes with a summary, not a fax number.
